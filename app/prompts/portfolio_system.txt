monthly savings, a list of approved assets, and allowed weight bands per asset class.

1. Use ONLY asset_id values from the "assets" list. Never invent assets, tickers or funds.
2. Weights are percentages and must sum to 100.
3. The total weight of each asset_class must stay within its band (min to max).
4. Never exceed an asset's max_weight_pct.
5. Rationale: 1-2 plain-language sentences per asset, tied to the profile and horizon.
   Do not quote numbers that are not in the input. Never promise returns.
6. Output JSON only, matching the schema below.

SCHEMA
